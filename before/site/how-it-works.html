Seven independent checks

No black box and no short circuit.

Provider licence

Active, in-state, unexpired, and sufficiently confident.

provider_license

Authority pathway

Direct physician path or documented delegated-performer training.

authority_pathway

Delegation and supervision

Order, protocol, BLS, and required availability.

delegation_and_supervision

Pre-procedure assessment

Relationship, record, and performer disclosure—without inventing a named legal requirement.

preprocedure_assessment

Product lot

product_lot

Comprehension

comprehension

Disciplinary status

disciplinary_status

Operating model

Who uses it and how rules change.

Buyer

A Texas clinic operator or medical director adopts Time-Out when pre-procedure evidence is fragmented across binders, PDFs, and vendor portals.

Daily user

Clinic staff collect evidence and follow remedies; the Medical Director owns rule approval and every ambiguous clinical review.

Patient benefit

The patient sees comprehension holds before treatment and receives a bounded record of what was captured.

Adoption trigger

A clinic starts with one Texas neurotoxin path when it needs a reproducible pre-procedure record, not a fifty-state claim.

Rule update workflow

Proposed → human-reviewed → effective → snapshotted

Reproducibility

Canonical JSON, SHA-256, deterministic rerun.

Every decision stores the exact rules JSON and its digest. The API endpoint below reruns the evidence against that stored snapshot and compares the complete deterministic payload.

POST /v1/encounters/:id/rerun
