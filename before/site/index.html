Some treatments cannot be undone.

Before every incision, a surgical team stops and confirms the patient, the site and the procedure out loud. The 13,000 US med spas injecting neurotoxin thousands of times a day have no equivalent, and when their malpractice cases reach a verdict, the leading causes aren’t bad hands. Time-Out is that pause. It checks who is performing the procedure, what is being used and whether the patient understood, and refuses to produce a safety record when the evidence isn’t there. Watch it refuse one:

What just happened

One encounter. Three kinds of proof.

Who may perform this

Texas doesn't publish a list of job titles allowed to inject. It requires documented delegation: specific training, a patient-specific order, a signed protocol, and an available supervisor. A title alone is never enough, so Time-Out never treats one as an answer.

WHAT The Gate checks the documented authority pathway, not the person's title alone.

WHY Missing delegation evidence holds the entire encounter for human review.

What is being used

The product lot is captured and checked against current alert candidates. In April 2026 the FDA warned a Texas med spa that couldn't reconcile the authentic Botox it purchased against what it recorded administering.

WHY An unverified or alerted lot blocks the safety record until a human confirms it.

Who this is for

Four people, one record.

The clinic

Gets a refusal it can act on: the exact missing document, the rule behind it, and a remedy, instead of discovering the gap in a deposition two years later.

The injector

Knows before the appointment whether they are covered for this procedure, on this patient, under this delegation. No judgement call in the moment.

The patient

Leaves with a record of what was checked and a baseline of their own skin from before anything was done, verifiable without trusting the clinic or us.

The Medical Director

Sees only what genuinely needs a human: the uncertain extraction, the ambiguous rule, the new alert. Everything else is already decided by cited code.

Where it would sit. Beside the booking system, not inside it, the check has to be able to say no to the software that scheduled the appointment. The unit of value is one refusal that would otherwise have become a claim.

Could this be a company

Somebody already pays for this. Badly, and afterwards.

Every refusal Time-Out produces is a claim that does not get filed. That is not a saving we have to explain, the industry already prices it.

Who buys it

The medical director who signs the delegation, and the malpractice carrier behind them. Carriers already discount premiums for documented risk-management programmes in medicine; this is one, with an audit trail a carrier can read.

What it replaces

A binder, a scanned PDF, and someone’s memory. In the twenty reviewed med-spa malpractice cases, thirteen ended in verdicts for the patient averaging near $2.5M, driven by absent consent, unexplained risk and unsupervised delegates. All three are checks in this Gate.

How it is priced

Per encounter, at a fraction of the per-procedure margin, or paid by the carrier as a premium credit. One prevented claim covers a clinic for years, which is why this is a plausible line item rather than another subscription to cancel.

Why now

Texas adopted new rules in January 2025. The FDA issued a warning letter to a Texas med spa in April 2026. Boards are tightening while the number of venues grows; enforcement is arriving in a market that has no shared standard of proof.

How it grows without re-engineering. The Gate is deterministic code over a cited rules table, so a new state is a rule-authoring job for a licensed reviewer, not a rewrite. Texas neurotoxin is the wedge because it is the highest-volume irreversible procedure with the clearest delegation law. Filler, then the next state, then the same shape of problem in dentistry and dermatology.

What we have not proven. No clinic has used this. Every patient, licence and lot here is synthetic, deliberately, so the demand evidence is case law, FDA enforcement and state rulemaking, not customers. The next step is not more features: it is one clinic running one week in shadow mode against their existing paperwork, which tells us whether the refusals are the right refusals. In the meantime, every assumption, edge case and failure mode is written down and pinned by a named test, read the whole list.

Built with

Nine tools. Each one owns a handoff.

Nothing here is decorative. Open a row to see what it does, how we used it, and why nothing else would have done the job.

Xano The entire backend DEPLOYED
What
15 tables, the encounter state machine, the seven-check Gate, an append-only audit log, the public REST API, and the hosting for this page.
How
Written as XanoScript and pushed from the CLI, so the whole workspace is committed as code and reviewable in the repo. It also brokers live calls to two other tools server-side, so no key ever reaches your browser.
Why
This product is a state machine with an audit trail, so Xano is the system rather than a database sitting behind one. The Gate runs there as a pure function with no network access, which is why the same evidence produces a byte-identical verdict on our machine, yours, and the server.
Nutrient DWS Reads documents into typed fields LIVE · CACHED REPLAY
What
Turns credential, intake and product documents into typed fields, each with a confidence score and page coordinates. Redaction runs before anything leaves the vault.
How
Anything below the confidence floor stops the encounter and goes to a named Medical Director, who sees the uncertain value boxed on the source page at the coordinates DWS returned.
Why
Most extraction pipelines throw the confidence score away. We made it the thing that stops the workflow, and we spend the page coordinates on the human: the reviewer sees the uncertain value boxed on the original page rather than retyped into a form. The threshold lives in code, never in a prompt. A model reads; it never decides.
SerpApi Finds alerts after the fact LIVE ON DEMAND
What
Live search across FDA warning letters and Texas board actions.
How
A hit becomes an alert candidate that moves an already-prepared encounter back to human review. A person confirms or dismisses it, and that decision is audited.
Why
Almost everyone uses search to populate a screen. Here a search result can reverse a decision that was already made; it moves an encounter out of ready and back into human review. Our first real query returned the actual April 2026 warning letter to a Texas med spa. And it still never decides: a person confirms or dismisses, on the record.
Perfect Corp Captures the skin baseline LIVE · CACHED REPLAY
What
Twelve scored skin concerns, overlay masks and skin age, captured before treatment.
How
Run once on a synthetic face and attached to the patient’s receipt, so they keep the record of how they looked first.
Why
Skin analysis is normally used to sell a treatment. We run it in the opposite direction: before anything is done, once, and the output belongs to the patient rather than the clinic. If something looks different in six weeks, there is a scored record of how it looked first. A baseline and a communication aid, never a diagnosis.
Foxit Assembles the record, then stops LIVE · SIGNED
What
An agent takes a plain-English prompt and assembles a three-page evidence record, watermarked SYNTHETIC on every page. A second call locates tampering after the fact.
How
Through the official MCP server, then it stops. A named human signed the attestation through eSign; the agent read the outcome back with GET requests only, and can never act on an envelope.
Why
An agent that could sign its own attestation would make the attestation worthless. The pause is the entire point.
name.com Gives each clinic the domain its patients verify against LIVE · 4 SURFACES
What
Domain search, availability, registration and DNS. All four, each doing work the product depends on.
How
Onboarding a clinic searches for a domain that reads as theirs, confirms it is actually purchasable before anyone is quoted a price, registers it, then publishes each sealed receipt’s SHA-256 as a TXT record underneath it and reads it back through the API. Registration is the one irreversible step, so it never runs from a page a visitor can click.
Why
Publishing every receipt under a domain we control leaves the patient trusting us, the exact thing the receipt exists to remove. Giving the clinic its own domain takes us out of the trust path entirely. A registrar is usually where a project starts; here it is what makes the last step verifiable. Stated plainly, because it matters: sandbox registrations do not resolve publicly and a TXT record stays mutable by whoever owns the domain. A verification channel, not a notary.
Doctavian Compiles consent that changes with the delegation LIVE · DOCUMENT GENERATED
What
One template, not a library of them. It branches on the authority pathway, because a physician injecting and a nurse injecting under delegation produce genuinely different consent, then loops over only the disclosures the Gate actually cited for that encounter. Patient and injector both sign.
How
The whole chain runs live: template upload, data upload, generate, download. It returns a two-page PDF with the encounter merged, the cited disclosures indexed out of the repeated record, and the disclosure count calculated by Doctavian rather than by us. Their markup elements are authored through an Office add-in and cannot be produced programmatically, so the Gate resolves the authority pathway from cited rules and passes the sentence in as data. That is the better split anyway: a document template is the wrong place to decide who may lawfully perform a procedure.
Proof
Open the document Doctavian generated. Two pages, every expression resolved, the disclosure count calculated on their platform.
Why
Every other consent form in this industry is a static PDF that says the same thing no matter who picks up the needle. Consent that is computed from who is actually performing the procedure, citing only the rules that applied, is the difference between a signature and a record. The refusal to over-scope is the product working as designed. It is the same instinct that makes the Gate say no.
Model Context Protocol How the agent holds its tools LIVE
What
The assembly agent reaches Foxit through the official MCP server rather than hand-rolled HTTP.
How
Three field-mapping bugs in that server surfaced during the build. Each is worked around through REST with the reason recorded in the run log, and all three were reported upstream with reproductions.
Why
Using a tool properly includes reporting it when it is wrong.
WebCrypto Reproducibility in your browser LIVE
What
Your browser fetches a live verdict, hashes the canonical ruleset itself, and compares it to the fingerprint the server recorded.
How
No library and no server round-trip for the hash, see it on How it works.
Why
You should not have to take our word for it.

Credentials live in Xano environment variables. None are in the repository, and none have ever been committed.

What this is not

We'd rather you trust the parts that work.

  • It does not determine legality. It produces a safety determination for a licensed human to review.
  • It does not certify that a treatment is safe, that a product is authentic, or that an outcome will be good.
  • It does not replace professional judgement. Every ambiguous call goes to a person, by design.
  • Teach-back isn't ours. What we haven't found documented elsewhere is binding a scored comprehension result to a whole-encounter hold with a versioned rule snapshot.
  • The receipt is a record, not a notary. It proves which checks and sources were captured before the procedure.
  • Texas neurotoxin only. Other states are a rule-authoring exercise, not a claim we've already made.
