Who may perform this

Texas doesn't publish a list of job titles allowed to inject. It requires documented delegation — specific training, a patient-specific order, a signed protocol, and an available supervisor. A title alone is never enough, so Time-Out never treats one as an answer.

WHAT The Gate checks the documented authority pathway, not the person's title alone.

WHY Missing delegation evidence holds the entire encounter for human review.

What this is not

We'd rather you trust the parts that work.

  • It does not determine legality. It produces a safety determination for a licensed human to review.
  • It does not certify that a treatment is safe, that a product is authentic, or that an outcome will be good.
  • It does not replace professional judgement. Every ambiguous call goes to a person, by design.
  • Teach-back isn't ours. What we haven't found documented elsewhere is binding a scored comprehension result to a whole-encounter hold with a versioned rule snapshot.
  • The receipt is a record, not a notary. It proves which checks and sources were captured before the procedure.
  • Texas neurotoxin only. Other states are a rule-authoring exercise, not a claim we've already made.
