Texas · neurotoxin · synthetic demonstration

Some treatments cannot be undone.

Before every incision, a surgical team stops and confirms the patient, the site and the procedure out loud. The 13,000 US med spas injecting neurotoxin thousands of times a day have no equivalent — and when their malpractice cases reach a verdict, the leading causes aren’t bad hands. They’re absent consent, unexplained risk and unsupervised delegates: paperwork failures, every one of them before the needle.

Time-Out is that pause. It checks who is performing the procedure, what is being used and whether the patient understood — and refuses to produce a safety record when the evidence isn’t there. Watch it refuse one:

What just happened

One encounter. Three kinds of proof.

Who may perform this

Texas doesn't publish a list of job titles allowed to inject. It requires documented delegation — specific training, a patient-specific order, a signed protocol, and an available supervisor. A title alone is never enough, so Time-Out never treats one as an answer.

WHAT The Gate checks the documented authority pathway, not the person's title alone.

WHY Missing delegation evidence holds the entire encounter for human review.

SOURCE 22 TAC §169.25 · TX_NEUROTOXIN_2025_01_09

What is being used

The product lot is captured and checked against current alert candidates. In April 2026 the FDA warned a Texas med spa that couldn't reconcile the authentic Botox it purchased against what it recorded administering.

WHAT A search result is an alert candidate, not a finding of authenticity or wrongdoing.

WHY An unverified or alerted lot blocks the safety record until a human confirms it.

SOURCE FDA warning letter · 1 Apr 2026 · product_lot

Whether the patient understood

A signature proves someone clicked. Time-Out asks the patient to say the risks back in their own words, and holds the encounter until they can.

WHAT Teach-back records whether a patient can explain the material in their own words.

WHY A signature without demonstrated understanding is not enough evidence for this safety record.

How it works

A cited checklist that cannot be skipped.

Time-Out is not an AI that decides what's legal. It's a checklist that cannot be skipped, built from cited rules, that freezes exactly what it checked.

Who this is for

Four people, one record.

The clinic

Gets a refusal it can act on — the exact missing document, the rule behind it, and a remedy — instead of discovering the gap in a deposition two years later.

The injector

Knows before the appointment whether they are covered for this procedure, on this patient, under this delegation. No judgement call in the moment.

The patient

Leaves with a record of what was checked and a baseline of their own skin from before anything was done — verifiable without trusting the clinic or us.

The Medical Director

Sees only what genuinely needs a human: the uncertain extraction, the ambiguous rule, the new alert. Everything else is already decided by cited code.

Where it would sit. Beside the booking system, not inside it — the check has to be able to say no to the software that scheduled the appointment. The unit of value is one refusal that would otherwise have become a claim.

Built with

Nine tools. Each one owns a handoff.

Nothing here is decorative. Open a row to see what it does, how we used it, and why nothing else would have done the job.

Xano The entire backend DEPLOYED
What
15 tables, the encounter state machine, the seven-check Gate, an append-only audit log, the public REST API, and the hosting for this page.
How
Written as XanoScript and pushed from the CLI, so the whole workspace is committed as code and reviewable in the repo. It also brokers live calls to two other tools server-side, so no key ever reaches your browser.
Why
This product is a state machine with an audit trail. Xano is the system, not a database sitting behind one.
Nutrient DWS Reads documents into typed fields LIVE · CACHED REPLAY
What
Turns credential, intake and product documents into typed fields, each with a confidence score and page coordinates. Redaction runs before anything leaves the vault.
How
Anything below the confidence floor stops the encounter and goes to a named Medical Director, who sees the uncertain value boxed on the source page at the coordinates DWS returned.
Why
The threshold lives in code, never in a prompt. A model reads; it never decides.
SerpApi Finds alerts after the fact LIVE ON DEMAND
What
Live search across FDA warning letters and Texas board actions.
How
A hit becomes an alert candidate that moves an already-prepared encounter back to human review. A person confirms or dismisses it, and that decision is audited.
Why
Live data with a consequence: it changes the workflow. Our first real search returned the April 2026 warning letter to a Texas med spa.
Perfect Corp Captures the skin baseline LIVE · CACHED REPLAY
What
Twelve scored skin concerns, overlay masks and skin age, captured before treatment.
How
Run once on a synthetic face and attached to the patient’s receipt, so they keep the record of how they looked first.
Why
An objective, repeatable starting point. A baseline and a communication aid — never a diagnosis.
Foxit Assembles the record, then stops LIVE · SIGNED
What
An agent takes a plain-English prompt and assembles a three-page evidence record, watermarked SYNTHETIC on every page. A second call locates tampering after the fact.
How
Through the official MCP server, then it stops. A named human signed the attestation through eSign; the agent read the outcome back with GET requests only, and can never act on an envelope.
Why
An agent that could sign its own attestation would make the attestation worthless. The pause is the entire point.
name.com Publishes the receipt fingerprint LIVE ON DEMAND
What
Domain search, availability, registration and DNS. Each receipt’s SHA-256 is published as a TXT record and read back through the API.
How
The patient can look the record up themselves, without an account here.
Why
Verification that does not require trusting our server. Stated plainly: sandbox DNS does not propagate publicly and a TXT record is mutable by its owner — a verification channel, not a notary.
Doctavian Compiles the consent document PARTIAL — see limits
What
One consent template that branches on the authority pathway and loops over cited disclosures. Patient and injector both sign.
How
Everything upstream of generation is live. Generation itself is blocked: delivery requires granting full Google Drive access, and we declined to hand a third party blanket Drive scope for a hackathon build. We asked for an internal-storage option.
Why
Consent that branches on who is performing the procedure is the difference between a form and a record. We would rather ship this honestly incomplete than overstate it.
Model Context Protocol How the agent holds its tools LIVE
What
The assembly agent reaches Foxit through the official MCP server rather than hand-rolled HTTP.
How
Three field-mapping bugs in that server surfaced during the build. Each is worked around through REST with the reason recorded in the run log, and all three were reported upstream with reproductions.
Why
Using a tool properly includes reporting it when it is wrong.
WebCrypto Reproducibility in your browser LIVE
What
Your browser fetches a live verdict, hashes the canonical ruleset itself, and compares it to the fingerprint the server recorded.
How
No library and no server round-trip for the hash — see it on How it works.
Why
You should not have to take our word for it.

Credentials live in Xano environment variables. None are in the repository, and none have ever been committed.

What this is not

We'd rather you trust the parts that work.

  • It does not determine legality. It produces a safety determination for a licensed human to review.
  • It does not certify that a treatment is safe, that a product is authentic, or that an outcome will be good.
  • It does not replace professional judgement. Every ambiguous call goes to a person, by design.
  • Teach-back isn't ours. What we haven't found documented elsewhere is binding a scored comprehension result to a whole-encounter hold with a versioned rule snapshot.
  • The receipt is a record, not a notary. It proves which checks and sources were captured before the procedure.
  • Texas neurotoxin only. Other states are a rule-authoring exercise, not a claim we've already made.
